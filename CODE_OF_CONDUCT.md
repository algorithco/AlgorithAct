# Code of Conduct

AlgorithAct welcomes people with different backgrounds, skill levels and viewpoints. These expectations apply to issues, PRs, reviews and other project spaces.

- Be respectful and constructive. Critique code and ideas, and explain how to improve them.
- Give context, listen to corrections and make room for newcomers.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, post sexualized material or publish private information.
- Do not spam project spaces or use them to target individuals.
- Respect ownership and licensing of code, artwork and media.

Maintainers may remove harmful content or restrict participation. They should apply these expectations fairly and explain decisions when doing so does not expose private information.

Report misconduct to a maintainer through a contact method they have made public on their GitHub profile. If a report involves a maintainer, contact another Algorithco owner. Share only information needed to investigate; do not post sensitive reports publicly. GitHub's abuse reporting tools remain available.
